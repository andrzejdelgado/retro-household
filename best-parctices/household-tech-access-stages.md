# Tech Access Stages

A per-technology policy for children aged 0 to 8, with markers for what comes later. It follows the strictest credible guidance where sources disagree: the French expert panel and Sweden's public health agency for screens, the American Academy of Pediatrics for video calls, and the EU Kids Act thresholds for AI and social platforms as a floor, then tightened where development evidence supports it.

In practice there is no tablet in the house for a child under 8. The only screens a child meets before 8 are a TV in a common room, a retro console plugged into it, a parent's phone for video calls, and whatever school mandates.

## Ground rules that override every bracket

- **Total screen budget** is a hard ceiling across every screen technology combined. Video calls and screen-free audio do not count. The weekly cap wins over the daily cap.

| Age | Per day | Per week | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 to 3 | 0 | 0 | never |
| 3 to 5 | 30 min | 2 h | max 4 days a week |
| 5 to 7 | 45 min | 4 h | max 5 days a week |
| 7 to 8 | 60 min | 5 h | max 6 days a week |

- **Screen-free zones and times** apply at every age: bedrooms, meals, the hour before sleep, before school or nursery, car trips under an hour, and restaurants.
- **Never as a tool.** No screen to calm a tantrum, to reward, to punish, or to occupy a child while a parent does something else. This single rule matters more than any duration.
- **No accounts, no ads, no autoplay, no recommendations, no in-app purchases** on anything a child touches before 8. Content is owned or downloaded, chosen and pre-watched by a parent.
- **Background screens count.** A TV on in the room is screen time for a toddler even when nobody is watching.
- **Parents model it.** No phones at the table, no phone during play or pick-up. A child under 3 should not see a parent scrolling.
- **Siblings.** Each child has their own policy. In a shared room the stricter policy applies. An older child's screen time happens where the younger cannot see it.

## Per-technology brackets

### Video calls

With known people, on a parent's device.

| Age | Depth | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 1 | Parent holds the device, known family only | 10 min per call, up to 3 a week |
| 1 to 3 | Same, child may talk and show things | 15 min per call, up to 4 a week |
| 3 to 8 | Child may start the call with a parent in the room | 20 min per call, up to 5 a week |
| Independent calls | No access before 12 | |

### Screen-free audio

Music, stories, audiobooks, an offline player with no mic or internet.

| Age | Depth | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 2 | Music and sung stories, chosen by parent, room speaker only | 30 min of recorded audio a day plus live singing, no headphones |
| 2 to 3 | Story recordings, parent present for first listens | 30 min a day, no headphones |
| 3 to 5 | Own offline player, child chooses from a parent-loaded library | 45 min a day, bedtime story allowed on top, volume-limited headphones for travel only |
| 5 to 8 | Adds podcasts pre-listened by a parent, audiobooks of any length | 60 min a day plus bedtime, up to 2 new titles a month added by parent |

### Watching long-form media

Films, series, documentaries, on a TV in a common room, no feeds.

| Age | Depth | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 3 | No access | 0 |
| 3 to 5 | Slow-paced, ad-free, offline, parent pre-watched, parent watching alongside for the whole session | 20 min per session, 30 min a day, 2 h a week, never two days in a row |
| 5 to 7 | Parent-chosen library, parent in the room for at least half, one episode per sitting, ends when the episode ends | 45 min a day, 4 h a week. One film of up to 90 min a week replaces that day's and part of the week's budget |
| 7 to 8 | Child picks from a parent-curated list of about 20 titles, parent nearby | 60 min a day, 5 h a week, one film up to 2 h a week, cinema up to once a month |
| Live TV with ads, streaming home page browsing | No access before 10 | |

### Short-form and algorithmic video

YouTube, YouTube Kids, Shorts, TikTok, Reels.

| Age | Depth | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 8 | No access. If one specific YouTube video is needed, the parent downloads it, plays it on the TV, and it counts as long-form | 0 |
| YouTube with recommendations | No access before 13 | |
| TikTok, Reels, Shorts | No access before 16 | |

### Tablet and mobile phone

Treated as one category.

| Age | Depth | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 8 | No personal or shared device. A parent's phone is used only for video calls as above | 0 |
| Basic phone, calls and texts with a parent-set contact list | No access before 11 | |
| Smartphone or tablet with internet | No access before 14 | |

### Games

Retro consoles such as NES or SNES, or their offline re-releases, on a TV in a common room.

| Age | Depth | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 6 | No digital games. Board games, cards, physical play | 0 |
| 6 to 7 | Offline, single-player, retro titles only, no chat, no ads, no purchases, no reward timers, parent has played it first | 20 min per session, 3 sessions a week, counted in the screen budget |
| 7 to 8 | Same. Titles added at most 1 a month | 30 min per session, 3 sessions a week, counted in the screen budget |
| Modern consoles, handhelds, mobile games, local multiplayer | No access before 10 | |
| Online multiplayer, in-game chat, Roblox, Fortnite, Minecraft servers | No access before 13 | |
| Loot boxes, gacha, in-game currency | No access in childhood | |

### Internet browsing and search

| Age | Depth | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 8 | No access. Questions the child asks are answered from books or by the parent looking it up alone | 0 |
| From 8 | With a parent, for a specific question the child asked, kid-safe search engine, parent types, parent reads results aloud | 10 min per session, 2 sessions a week, counted in the screen budget |
| Independent browsing | No access before 12 | |

### Voice assistants and smart speakers

| Age | Depth | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 16 | No access. No device in the child's spaces. Household devices muted when the child is present, and the child does not address them | 0 |

### AI chatbots, companions, and generative tools

| Age | Depth | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 12 | No access. The child does not see a parent use one either | 0 |
| Supervised tool use for schoolwork | No access before 12 | |
| AI companions and character chatbots | No access in childhood, revisit at 16 at the earliest | |

### AI and connected toys

Anything with a microphone, camera, or cloud connection.

| Age | Depth | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 8 | No access. Offline audio players without a mic are the allowed alternative | 0 |

### Camera

A simple offline camera, no phone.

| Age | Depth | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 5 | No access | 0 |
| 5 to 8 | Basic offline camera, no editing apps, photos viewed on the camera or printed, nothing uploaded | Unlimited outdoors, not counted as screen time, 10 min a day reviewing shots |

### Kids' smartwatch

| Age | Depth | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 8 | No access | 0 |
| From 8 | Only if the child travels alone. Calls and location with a parent-set contact list, no games, no camera, no internet, no messaging, worn only for the journey | Journeys only, removed at home |

### Messaging and group chats

| Age | Depth | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 8 | No access. Messages to family go through the parent's phone with the parent typing | 0 |
| Own messaging with known contacts, parent can read | No access before 12 | |
| Group chats, class chats | No access before 13 | |

### Social media

| Age | Depth | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 8 | No access | 0 |
| Any platform with a feed, followers, or public profile | No access before 16 | |

### School devices and educational apps

| Age | Depth | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 6 | Parents ask nursery and preschool for a no-individual-screens policy and opt the child out where possible | 0 at home |
| 6 to 8 | Whatever school mandates in class. At home, only apps the school requires, on a parent's laptop in a common room, no gamified learning apps, paper alternatives requested | 20 min a day at home, counted in the screen budget |

### The child's digital footprint

What parents do, from birth.

| Age | Depth |
|---|---|
| 0 to 8 | No public posting of face, name, school, or location. Family sharing only through private channels. No accounts created in the child's name. No cloud cameras or connected monitors in the child's room. From age 5, the child is asked before a photo is shared with anyone |

## Progression and enforcement

- **Reveal dates, not drift.** Each technology is introduced on a fixed date the parents set in advance, not when the child asks or when a sibling gets it. The first two weeks in any new bracket are a trial at half the listed duration.
- **Readiness gates.** A child moves up only if, for the previous month, they stopped without a fight when time was up, slept normally, still chose books and play unprompted, and showed no change in mood or attention. Any one failing gate holds the child in the current bracket for another month.
- **Step back, then forward.** Meltdowns at switch-off, sleep disruption, sneaking, or a drop in reading or play returns the child to the previous bracket for four weeks. Screens are never removed as a punishment for unrelated behaviour.
- **Monthly review.** Parents check the week's actual minutes against the caps, the content library, and the readiness gates on a fixed day each month, and write the child's current bracket down where both parents can see it.
- **Conflicts resolve strict.** When two rules give different answers, the stricter one applies. When parents disagree, the stricter parent's reading applies until the next monthly review.
