# Household Rhythms

Weekday and weekend rhythm for a household with children aged 0 to 8. Infants set their own rhythm and follow the outdoor row in household-routine-elements.md. From 1 the household runs on a fixed rhythm, since predictability is what Kim John Payne and the Danish approach identify as the main protector against overwhelm. Times assume daycare or school from about 8 to 16. The screen slot, where tech-access-stages.md allows one, sits inside the 16:30 to 17:30 window and never after dinner.

## Weekday rhythm

| Time | 1 to 3 | 3 to 5 | 5 to 8 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 06:30 to 07:30 | Wake, breakfast | Same, child dresses self | Same, child makes own bed and packs bag |
| 07:30 | Walk or pram to daycare | Walk, bike or scooter | Walk or bike, alone from 6 or 7 |
| 16:00 | Pick-up, parent's phone stays in pocket. Home via playground 30 min | Same | Home alone or via playground 30 min |
| 16:30 to 17:30 | Free play. Parent transition ritual | Free play, chores 10 min | Free play, chores 15 min, reading 15 min |
| 17:30 to 18:00 | Dinner | Dinner | Dinner |
| 18:00 to 18:30 | Outside or free play | Outside or free play | Outside, game or one-on-one |
| 18:30 | Wind-down and bed | Wind-down and bed from 19:00 | Wind-down and bed from 19:30 |

## Weekend rhythm

| Time | 1 to 3 | 3 to 5 | 5 to 8 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning | No alarm, breakfast | Same | Same, reading hour on one morning |
| 09:30 to 12:00 | Outside: playground, forest, bike | Outside. Sunday nature outing | Outside. Sunday hike 2 to 3 h |
| 12:00 to 13:00 | Lunch at home | Lunch at home | Lunch at home |
| 13:00 to 15:00 | Nap | Quiet hour with books | Quiet hour, then free play |
| 15:00 to 17:00 | Free play, Saturday cleaning hour with the family | Same, peer play | Same, peer play, unsupervised outside |
| 17:00 to 18:00 | Dinner | Dinner | Dinner, Sunday family meeting 15 min |
| Friday evening | Cosy evening 60 min | Same | Same, board game |
| One weekend day | Nothing planned by adults | Same | Same |

## Open points

- The weekday rhythm assumes a 16:00 pick-up, the Nordic norm.
