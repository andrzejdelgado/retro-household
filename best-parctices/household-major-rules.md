# Household Major Rules

Rules for the adults and the home in a household with children aged 0 to 8. These sit above the practices in household-routine-elements.md and the brackets in tech-access-stages.md.

- **Phones go in a drawer** from pick-up until the child's bedtime on weekdays and during every meal at weekends. Parents check them in three fixed 10-minute slots. No phone in the bedroom overnight. No phone in hand while a child is speaking.
- **Work stops at pick-up.** No email or laptop between pick-up and bedtime. The laptop lives out of sight.
- **No television as the focal point** of the living room, and never on in the background. Music is fine.
- **Parents read where the child can see** for 15 min a day, and go outside when bored rather than reach for a screen.
- **Adults sleep 7 hours** and protect it. Parental stress and exhaustion predict worse child outcomes more reliably than most screen measures.
- **The home is set up for the child's independence.** Hooks, shelves and toys at child height. Outdoor gear by the door in every season. Art supplies and books reachable without asking.
- **One-on-one time is per child and per parent.** Siblings do not share it.
- **Both parents run the same rules.** Disagreements are settled away from the child, and the stricter reading holds until the monthly review.
- **Monthly review**, on the same day as the stages review: outdoor hours, whether the boredom rule held, whether chores and one-on-one time actually happened, and the child's mood and play.
